The Pressing format

A Pressing is a UTF-8 plain-text .deadwax file describing one repeating, instrumental loop: its harmony, bass, melody, drums, instruments, mix, surface textures and how far each musical dimension may drift. This page describes version 1 of the format. Dead Wax plays Pressings in the browser.

A complete short example

deadwax 1
title A quiet corner
tempo 76
meter 4/4
key C major
swing 0.16
bars 4
seed 104729

instrument chords epiano
instrument bass upright
instrument melody vibes
level chords 0.7
level bass 0.8
level melody 0.4
level drums 0.65

chords Dm9:4 G13:4 Cmaj7#11:4 Am9:4
bass auto root-fifth
melody -:2 A4:1 C5:1 B4:2 -:2 E5:4 -:4
kick X... ..g. x... ....
rim .... x... .... x..g
hats x.g. x.g. x.g. x.gg

texture vinyl 0.12
texture tape 0.05
texture rain 0.1

drift rate 0.35
drift tempo 60 96
drift swing 0 0.4
drift tone 0.15 0.85
drift busyness 0.1 0.8
drift vinyl 0 0.5

Lines and diagnostics

  • The first non-blank, non-comment line must be exactly deadwax 1.
  • Keywords, notes, instruments and chord symbols are case-sensitive.
  • One directive per line. Spaces/tabs separate tokens. Blank lines are ignored.
  • ; starts a full-line comment, or an inline comment when preceded by whitespace. # is not a comment character: it belongs to sharp notes and chord alterations.
  • Required lines: deadwax, title, tempo, meter, key, swing, bars, chords. Other lines have defaults.
  • Header and sequence lines cannot be duplicated. instrument, level, dimension, texture and drift may repeat for different settings; later settings for the same name win.
  • Unknown directives, instruments and tokens are errors, not silent fallbacks.
  • Errors identify a 1-based line and column. Token errors point to the token (or bad character); sequence-length/cross-field errors point to the line start. Missing required lines and invalid cross-field drift bounds point to line 1.

Header

Line Meaning
title Window seat, 1:17 am Unquoted title, remainder of the line
tempo 72 Quarter notes per minute, 45–130; decimals allowed
meter 4/4 Numerator 1–12; denominator 4 or 8
key C minor Root plus major, minor, dorian or mixolydian
swing 0.18 Odd sixteenths delayed by this fraction of a sixteenth, 0–0.48
bars 4 Loop length, integer 1–64
seed 104729 Unsigned 32-bit integer, default 104729 (see below)

Tempo always counts quarter notes, including 6/8. A 6/8 bar is three quarter notes, or twelve sixteenth-note steps. The key is descriptive metadata; explicit chords and notes are authoritative. Changing the key does not transpose them.

Chords and durations

chords Dm9:4 G13:4 Cmaj7#11:4 F#m7b5:2 Bb7alt:2

symbol:duration means a chord for that many quarter notes. Durations must be positive multiples of 0.25; the total must equal the complete loop length. Chords may span bars or change mid-bar. They are softly rearticulated at bar boundaries. A 4-bar 4/4 loop totals 16 quarter notes; 4 bars of 3/4 total 12.

Roots: A–G, optionally # or b. Supported qualities: 

(no suffix)  m  6  m6  7  maj7  m7  mmaj7
9  maj9  m9  11  m11  13  maj13  m13
m7b5  dim  dim7  aug  sus2  sus4  7sus4  add9  7alt  alt

Append alterations b5, #5, b9, #9, b11, #11, b13, #13, including several together, e.g. G7b9#5. Add a slash bass: Cmaj9/E, Abmaj9/Eb. Automatic bass respects the slash root; keyboard voicings remain rootless.

The voicing engine searches rootless/extension, shell, drop-2, quartal and upper-structure shapes and minimises motion from the previous chord. Density and register influence the search; seeded variation avoids a completely mechanical repetition. Explicit extensions are retained in the pitch pool. This is a compact voicing engine, not a comprehensive jazz-theory or arbitrary chord-symbol interpreter.

Explicit voicings

chords Dm9:4 G13:4 Cmaj9:8
chord-notes F3+A3+C4+E4:4 F3+B3+E4+A4:4 E3+G3+B3+D4:8

chord-notes overrides the voicing search. It must have exactly the same number of events and durations as chords. -:4 means a silent chord event. Dead Wax's Capture writes these, so a captured take keeps its exact notes and gaps.

Bass and melody

bass auto roots
bass auto root-fifth
bass auto walking

Choose one. Roots plays half-note pulses; root-fifth alternates at quarter notes; walking uses roots/thirds/fifths and chromatic approaches to the next bar. Notes stay in a bass register. Default: auto roots.

Or write explicit notes:

bass C2:2 G2:2 A1:2 E2:2
melody E4:1 G4:0.5 A4:0.5 -:2

Notes are A–G, optional #/b, octave 0–8 (C4 is middle C). - is a rest. Use + for simultaneous pitches, e.g. C4+Eb4:1. Each explicit sequence must have a total duration that divides the complete loop exactly. Shorter sequences repeat. Omit melody for no motif; Drift can add chord-tone fragments if embellishment rises. Explicit sequences can sustain across bar lines.

Drums

One line per voice: kick, snare, rim, hats, brushes, perc. 

kick X... ..g. x... .... | X... .... x..g ..g.
rim .... x... .... x..g
hats x.g. x.g. x.g. x.gg

Each character is one sixteenth note. Spaces are cosmetic. | separates bars; there are no leading/trailing pipes. Each grid bar must contain exactly the meter's number of sixteenths (16 for 4/4, 12 for 3/4 or 6/8).

Mark Meaning Velocity
. Rest 0
x Normal hit 0.68
X Accent 1
g Ghost note 0.24

The number of written grid bars must divide the loop length. A single bar repeats throughout. Missing voices are silent (but Drift can introduce ghost hits).

Instruments, mix and muting

instrument chords epiano
instrument bass upright
instrument melody vibes
level chords 0.7
level bass 0.8
level melody 0.4
level drums 0.65
mute melody drums

Melodic instruments: epiano (FM tine), felt (muted additive piano), upright (plucked triangle/body), vibes (inharmonic metallic partial), pad (detuned, filtered soft triangles). Any melodic instrument can be used on any melodic part. Defaults are as above, except the default melody level is 0.45.

The drum kit is procedural too: pitched envelopes, filtered noise and resonant clicks. perc is a soft falling wood-like tone. Mix levels are 0–1. Omit mute for no mutes. Master volume is a listener setting, not part of the Pressing.

The master chain has kick ducking, low-pass tone, tape-like saturation, optional bit-depth quantisation (dimension crush), dual-rate modulated delay for wow and flutter, and a seeded stereo convolution reverb. Everything is generated in the browser, including the reverb impulse.

Textures

texture vinyl 0.12
texture surface 0.04
texture tape 0.05
texture rumble 0.06
texture rain 0.2
texture room 0.03
texture hum 0.02

All levels are 0–1. vinyl combines seeded short pops with density proportional to level. surface and tape are differently filtered hiss; rumble is a pair of sub-bass tones; rain is filtered noise plus occasional drops; room is dark noise; hum uses 50 Hz and harmonics. Defaults: vinyl 0.12, tape 0.05, all others zero.

Drift

Drift lets the player wander away from the score and back. Each musical dimension starts at its origin from the Pressing. Only explicitly permitted dimensions move: add drift NAME LOW HIGH bounds for each one. 

drift rate 0.35
drift tempo 58 100
drift tone 0.1 0.9
drift reverb 0.1 0.75
drift rain 0 0.5

Origins must lie inside the bounds. Limits must be inside the absolute range. Omitting a dimension from drift locks it at home. Set initial non-header, non-texture dimensions with dimension NAME VALUE:

Dimension Absolute range Default Effect
tempo 45–130 header Quarter-note rate, smoothed each step
swing 0–0.48 header Sixteenth-note swing
humanise 0–1 0.18 Small seeded timing offsets
busyness 0–1 0.35 Add ghost hits or thin normal hits relative to origin
embellish 0–1 0.1 Add motif neighbours/chord tones relative to origin
density 0–1 0.55 Keyboard voicing size
register 0–1 0.45 Keyboard pitch centre
substitution 0–1 0 Chance of dominant tritone substitution
rhythm 0–1 0 Mid-bar chord rearticulation above 0.25
timbre 0–1 0 Chord instrument: score below 0.35, felt, then pad above 0.72
tone 0–1 0.48 Master low-pass brightness
saturation 0–1 0.25 Soft tape drive
wow 0–1 0.2 Wow/flutter delay depth
crush 0–1 0 Optional bit-depth reduction (16 down to 5 bits)
reverb 0–0.85 0.24 Wet room/reverb level
balance 0–1 0.5 Bass versus melody emphasis
arrangement 0–1 0 Probability of resting chords/melody for a bar
each texture name 0–1 see above Texture level, also a drift dimension

drift rate (0–1) sets how far and how fast it roams at first; the listener can change it while playing. The walk updates once per bar. Notes, patterns and instrument changes happen at bar boundaries; filter, mix, effects and tempo glide.

Seed and reproducibility

seed initialises a 32-bit Mulberry-style generator. Score planning, timing and sound/noise generation use separate derived streams. The same seed, Pressing and the same bar-timed steering reproduce the same musical session; a seed alone cannot encode a listener's actions. Browser and sample-rate differences can still change the exact waveform.

Canonical form

Dead Wax keeps exported text unchanged, including comments. Its canonical serialiser (used by Capture) normalises spacing and numbers and drops comments, but parse → serialise → parse preserves the musical score. There is no audio export; a Pressing is always text.
